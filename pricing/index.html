Requirements

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11

    Escribal is a desktop application and is installed from the Microsoft Store.

  • A connection to check the subscription

    Once per working session, and for whatever your workflow asks for.

Escribal is not available in the European Union or in China for now.

The subscription

How it works

  • Bought inside the program

    The download is free and the subscription is bought from the program's start window, through the Microsoft Store. No account with the author and no sign-in are needed: the purchase uses the Microsoft account already signed in to Windows.

  • Microsoft sells it and charges for it

    Price, taxes, renewal, cancellation and refunds follow the Microsoft Store terms.

  • There is no free trial

    To see what the program does before subscribing, the example shows a whole workflow and the guides explain how one is built.

  • Without a subscription, nothing runs

    Without an active subscription the program runs no workflow, not even when an AI assistant launches it.

  • What you have made stays yours

    When the subscription ends, the documents you generated and your .scriba files stay on your computer.
